### **Prompt for Gemini Video Generation**

**[Objective]**
Create a comprehensive video generation prompt for Korean Sign Language (KSL) videos based on the provided sentence analysis and sign language data.

**[Input Data]**
- **Sentence Analysis**: {{sentence_analysis}}
- **Sign Language Data**: {{sign_data}}

**[Procedure and Rules]**

1. **Prompt Structure**: Create a detailed video generation prompt that includes:
   - Clear video requirements (duration, aspect ratio, lighting)
   - Specific sign language instructions
   - Facial expression and body language guidance
   - Technical specifications for video quality
   - figure image reference for consistent appearance

2. **Sign Language Integration**: 
   - Include ONLY the sign language data that was successfully retrieved from the Culture API
   - For each word with sign data, provide the specific sign description
   - Do NOT include generic descriptions for words without sign data
   - Focus on accuracy and cultural authenticity

3. **Video Requirements**:
   - Duration: 5-8 seconds
   - Aspect ratio: 16:9
   - Lighting: Bright and clear for hand visibility
   - figure appearance: Based on provided reference image

4. **Sign Language Guidelines**:
   - Natural and fluent signing movements
   - Clear hand gestures and facial expressions
   - Appropriate emotional expression matching the sentence content
   - Consistent signing speed throughout the video
   - Smooth transitions between signs

**[Output Format]**
Generate a comprehensive, detailed prompt that can be directly used by Veo3 video generation API. The prompt should be in English and include all necessary details for creating a high-quality Korean Sign Language video with figure consistency.

**[Example Structure]**
```
Create a sign language video showing the figure from the provided reference image performing Korean Sign Language (KSL)

Video Requirements:
- Show the figure from the reference image
- **Consistency in the figure's appearance and sign language must be maintained for the full duration of the video.**
- The figure's hands and fingers should follow the sign language movements precisely
- Clear, natural hand movements and facial expressions matching the figure's appearance
- Appropriate facial expressions that match the content and tone of the sentence
- Duration: 5-8 seconds
- Good lighting with clear visibility of hands and face

Sign Language Details:
[Include only actual sign data from Culture API]

Key Instructions:
1. The figure should appear natural and fluent in their signing
2. Hand movements should be clear and precise
3. Facial expressions should convey the meaning appropriately
4. Maintain consistent signing speed throughout
5. Ensure smooth transitions between signs
6. The figure should maintain appropriate eye contact with the camera
7. Focus solely on the figure's sign language performance
8. **From start to finish, the figure must continuously and consistently perform the same sign language throughout the entire video.**
9. CRITICAL: **Only the background, figure, and hand movements should be present. No other elements.**
10. CRITICAL: **The video should feature only the specified figure.**

Additional Context:
Please create a video of the figure performing Korean Sign Language interpretation for this content
```

- Use ONLY the sign language data provided
- Do NOT invent or guess sign descriptions
- Focus on creating a prompt that will result in a high-quality, authentic KSL video
- Ensure the prompt is specific enough for Veo3 to generate appropriate content
